# Job Market Analysis in Tableau 

## Project Overview
This project analyzes job market trends in the Data & Analytics industry using Tableau. The goal is to uncover insights that help recruiters (DataSearch) understand demand for roles, required skills, salary trends, and industry behavior.

The dataset consists of job postings over the past 5 years, including attributes like job title, industry, salary, experience, applicants, and required skills.

---

## Objectives
- Identify the most in-demand job roles
- Analyze required skills across job titles
- Explore salary trends based on experience
- Discover industry-wise hiring patterns
- Build interactive dashboards for recruiters

---

## Dataset Overview
- 5 years of job posting data
- 19 columns including:
  - Job Title
  - Company Industry
  - Job Type & Level
  - Skills Required
  - Salary (Min & Max)
  - Years of Experience
  - Number of Applicants (first 24 hrs)

⚠️ Note:  
- Over 90% of minimum salary values are missing  
- Dataset is fictitious (used for analysis practice only)

---

## Key Findings

### Job Market Trends
- Job postings are **increasing over time**
- A **sharp decline in March 2020** due to COVID-19
- Market quickly recovered after initial drop

---

### Most In-Demand Roles
- **Data Engineers** → Highest demand
- **Data Scientists** → Second highest number of job postings
- **Data Analysts** → Highly востребован across industries

---

### Industry Insights
- Total distinct industries: **119**
- **Computer Software** is the **3rd most popular industry** by total applicants
- Multiple industries actively hiring data roles

---

### Skills Analysis
- **Tableau** is one of the top required skills
- **Data Analyst roles** list Tableau most frequently in top 3 skills
- Strong correlation between job roles and specific skill requirements

---

### Applicants Insights
- Total applicants for jobs requiring Tableau: **27,335**
- Senior-level roles receive **highest number of applications**

---

### Experience Insights
- Internship roles require:
  - **0.37 years (~20 weeks)** of experience → lowest requirement
- Executive-level Data Science roles require:
  - **8.33 years (average)** experience

---

### Salary Insights
- Salary data has **many missing values**, limiting analysis
- **Machine Learning Engineers (8 years experience)**:
  - Average salary: **$137,500**
- Salaries increase significantly with experience

---

## Key Insights Summary
- Data roles are rapidly growing in demand
- Data Engineers dominate hiring trends
- Tableau is a critical skill, especially for analysts
- Senior roles attract more applicants
- Experience directly impacts salary
- Industry demand is highly diversified

---

## Recommendations

### For Recruiters (DataSearch)
- Focus hiring efforts on:
  - Data Engineers
  - Data Scientists
  - Data Analysts
- Target high-demand industries like Computer Software
- Prioritize candidates with Tableau and analytics skills
- Prepare for high competition in senior roles

---

### For Job Seekers
- Learn **Tableau** to increase employability
- Focus on high-demand roles:
  - Data Engineering
  - Data Science
- Gain experience to unlock higher salary opportunities
- Apply early to jobs (high competition within 24 hours)

---

### For Companies
- Provide salary transparency to improve applicant quality
- Optimize job postings during stable market periods
- Clearly define required skills to attract suitable candidates

---

## Limitations
- Missing salary data affects accuracy
- Dataset is fictitious (not real-world applicable)
- Skill data may not reflect full job requirements
